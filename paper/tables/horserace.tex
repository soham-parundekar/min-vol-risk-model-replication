\notetext{Each estimator is run through the same constrained optimiser on the same rebalance calendar, so the rows differ only in $\Sigma$. The four realised volatilities span 25 basis points. Pred./real. is the ratio of mean predicted to realised annualised volatility, and bias $b$ is the standard deviation of realised return divided by the previous period's predicted standard deviation, computed on the minimum-variance portfolio over 748 observations. The two measure calibration differently and are not interchangeable: $b$ carries a well-specified band of [0.948, 1.052], and all four estimators sit above it. Turnover is the mean one-way figure per rebalance. Loosened counts the rebalances of 29 at which the published ladder put a weaker bound in force, and No trade those at which the ladder was exhausted without reaching a feasible book, so the previous weights were carried forward unchanged. The turnover cap is what binds in almost every case; the sector band was never the bound that had to be loosened.}
